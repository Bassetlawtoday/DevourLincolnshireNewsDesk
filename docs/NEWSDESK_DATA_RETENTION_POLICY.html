NewsDesk Pro Data Retention Policy

Owner: Devour Lincolnshire · System: NewsDesk Pro · Version: 1.2 — 31 August 2026 · Review: annually and after material change

This policy explains what the newsroom system stores, where, why, for how long, and how it is disposed of. It is an operational policy; seek professional advice where formal legal assurance is required.

Purpose and scope

NewsDesk Pro is an internal editorial research and production system covering Planning, Police, Fire, Sport, Council, Events, Local Democracy Intelligence and Social Desk. Information is used only for legitimate newsroom discovery, verification, analysis, selection, production, provenance and publication, subject to source licences, copyright, data-protection law, journalism protections and the public interest.

Safeguards

  1. Only necessary fields are retained; unnecessary LDRS contact emails are removed.
  2. Newsletter-selected material is protected from automatic source-record deletion.
  3. Abandoned drafts are reviewed after 90 days and are not silently deleted.
  4. Non-identifying aggregate totals may outlive source detail if they cannot reconstruct content or identify a person.
  5. Retention does not grant republication rights; licence, embargo, caption, credit and permission terms still apply.
  6. Credentials and local source data are excluded from Git; access is restricted to authorised users.
  7. Corrections, takedowns and rights changes must be applied promptly.

Operation and accountability

Retention runs on application startup and on demand from the Data Retention Centre. Each run records its time, policy version, outcome and action counts without copying article content into the audit. The editor/data controller reviews legal holds, rights changes, requests, exceptions and backups. A legal hold must record its reason, scope, approver and review date.
